What it does

1 2 3 4 5 6
1

Match records and lifetime stats

Nine-ball, eight-ball, ten-ball and 5-9. Record your opponent, the score and the date, and see your overall wins and losses and your record against each player. Any result can be shared with your followers as a post.

2

5-9 score sheet

Score each rack on a sheet laid out like the paper one. Points differ from hall to hall and group to group, so you build and save your own rule sets. Handicaps are set per player. Two to six players.

3

Bowlliards

Tap the frames and the app carries strikes and spares forward for you. Shows your average, your best and a rough class.

The screenshots show the Japanese version.

  • 4

    My cues

    Keep your cues with photos and notes, including parts such as shafts and tips.

  • 5

    Favorite halls and check-ins

    “At ○○ at 8:14 pm” in one tap. Only the time is kept, so it never becomes untrue as time passes.

  • 6

    Follow with a QR code

    Just show your QR code and let your friend scan it. No mistyped names.

Our rules

  • GPS is used only to list nearby halls. No continuous or background location.
  • Check-ins are visible to your followers only, never to everyone.
  • Match records stay on your device until you make them a post.
  • Block, mute and report are built in, so you can remove anyone you don’t want to see.
